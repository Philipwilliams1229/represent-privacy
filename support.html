RePresent

Support

RePresent is a better presenter for iPad: the iPad shows your slides, notes and what's next, and the TV shows only the clean slide.

Need help?

Email represent.supportpage@gmail.com. Tell me which iPad you have, what you were doing, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Quick answers

  • How do I put a presentation in the app? Tap Import and pick a PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF file, or share a file to RePresent from the Files app or AirDrop.
  • How do I show the TV? Open Control Center on the iPad, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your Apple TV. The TV shows only the slide.
  • Keynote or old .ppt files? In that app, export or save as .pptx or PDF first.
  • The editor: presenting is free; the editor is a one-time purchase. Bought it before, or on another iPad? Tap Edit, then Restore Purchase. Have a code? Tap Redeem a Code on the same screen.
  • More help is inside the app under Settings › Help.
