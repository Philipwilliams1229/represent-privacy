RePresent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

In short: RePresent does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPad.

What the app is

RePresent is an iPad app for presenting slides. You add PowerPoint or PDF files (or make your own with the editor), and the app shows them on your iPad and, if you choose, on a TV or projector through AirPlay.

Information we collect

None. RePresent has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app never sends your presentations, notes or drawings anywhere, and it contains no code from third parties that collects information.

The editor purchase

The editor is a one-time in-app purchase. Buying it, restoring it and redeeming a code are handled entirely by Apple's App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. RePresent only learns whether the editor is unlocked on your iPad; it never sees your payment details or Apple Account.

YouTube videos and links

If a slide contains a YouTube video, it plays in YouTube's own embedded player, which connects to YouTube while it plays. To do this, RePresent uses YouTube API Services. YouTube's privacy policy applies to that player (Google Privacy Policy). While a YouTube video plays in the app, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Links on a slide open in Safari when you tap them. RePresent sends nothing about you to YouTube or to any website.

What stays on your device

  • The presentations you add, and any changes you make to them in the app.
  • Presenter notes, and your drawings if you turn on Save drawings in Settings.
  • Your app settings, such as pen colors and layout choices.

This information lives only inside the app on your iPad. We cannot see it. If you delete a presentation or delete the app, it is removed from your iPad.

Photos and files

If you add a picture, video or PDF to a slide, you choose it yourself through Apple's photo or file picker. The app only receives the files you pick, and does not ask for access to your whole photo library. When you export a presentation, you choose where the file goes.

AirPlay and your TV

When you mirror your iPad to an Apple TV or another AirPlay screen, the slide is sent to that screen by iPadOS itself. RePresent does not record or store what is shown.

Children

RePresent collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
