RePresent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In short: RePresent does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPad.

What the app is

RePresent is an iPad app for presenting slides in class. You add PowerPoint or PDF files, and the app shows them on your iPad and, if you choose, on a TV or projector through AirPlay.

Information we collect

None. RePresent has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet to send anything anywhere, and it contains no code from third parties that does.

What stays on your device

  • The presentations you add, and any changes you make to them in the app.
  • Presenter notes, and your drawings if you turn on Save drawings in Settings.
  • Your app settings, such as pen colors and layout choices.

This information lives only inside the app on your iPad. We cannot see it. If you delete a presentation or delete the app, it is removed from your iPad.

Photos and files

If you add a picture to a slide, you choose it yourself through Apple's photo or file picker. The app only receives the pictures and files you pick, and does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

AirPlay and your TV

When you mirror your iPad to an Apple TV or another AirPlay screen, the slide is sent to that screen by iPadOS itself. RePresent does not record or store what is shown.

Children

RePresent collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
